# Caption Framework v2.0 — Prisha Online Centre
# MANDATORY for all caption generation

═══════════════════════════════════════════

TARGET AUDIENCE:
- Maharashtra business owners, shopkeepers, entrepreneurs
- People needing: registrations, certificates, government schemes, 
  business compliance, CSC services, Maha e-Seva, online documentation
- Local, practical, trust-first mindset

TONE:
- Professional but human (like a 10-yr experienced local business)
- NOT influencer-style
- NOT generic AI marketing
- Trust → Benefits → Services (in that order)
- Bilingual: Marathi + English in EVERY post

═══════════════════════════════════════════

MANDATORY STRUCTURE (9 sections):

1️⃣ ATTENTION-GRABBING HOOK
   - 1-2 lines max
   - Stops scrolling
   Examples:
   "⚠️ Most business owners don't know this..."
   "❌ Don't make this costly registration mistake."
   "💰 You may be missing benefits worth thousands."
   "📢 Important update for shop owners and entrepreneurs."

2️⃣ ENGLISH EXPLANATION
   - 2-4 short lines
   - Focus: why it matters, who it affects, what benefit
   - Simple, clear, no jargon

3️⃣ MARATHI EXPLANATION
   - Same topic, natural Marathi
   - NOT a direct translation
   - Write as if talking to a local customer
   - Use Devanagari script

4️⃣ BENEFITS SECTION (checkmarks ✅)
   - Show outcomes, NOT features
   - 5-7 bullet points
   Examples:
   ✅ Faster approval process
   ✅ Government-recognized registration
   ✅ Better business credibility
   ✅ Access to applicable schemes
   ✅ Proper documentation support

5️⃣ WHY CHOOSE PRISHA
   - 4 trust-building points
   Examples:
   🤝 Reliable Guidance
   📄 Complete Documentation Support
   ⚡ Fast Process
   📞 Dedicated Assistance

6️⃣ CALL TO ACTION
   - ONE clear action
   Examples:
   📩 Send us a message
   📞 Contact us today
   💬 Comment "HELP"
   📱 WhatsApp us for details
